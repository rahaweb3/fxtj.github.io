About Us

Built by traders who got tired of spreadsheets.

FXTJ started as a messy Excel sheet shared between three prop-firm traders trying to figure out why their equity curves kept stalling at the same spot. The answer was never the strategy — it was the lack of an honest record of what actually happened. FXTJ is that record: fast to log, hard to lie to yourself in, and built around the accounts and rules real traders actually use.
